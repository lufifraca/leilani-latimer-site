Leadership values

How I Lead

My work is grounded in six values: curiosity, courage, culture, creativity, collaboration, and communication.

They shape how I build trust, make decisions, lead through complexity, and help organizations move from strategy to execution.

  1. 01

    Curiosity

    Embraces change with agility, confidence, and adaptability

    Leilani leads with an entrepreneurial mindset, using curiosity to fuel innovation, navigate ambiguity, and respond to market trends. She makes data-driven decisions, aligns strategy with customer needs, and cultivates a culture of continuous learning, adaptability, and accountability.

  2. 02

    Courage

    Makes bold decisions in complex, high-stakes environments

    Whether leading transformation or pursuing growth opportunities, Leilani approaches challenges with clarity and conviction. Courage means standing up for her values, asking tough questions, and acknowledging mistakes. She drives long-term impact through transparent and ethical decision-making.

  3. 03

    Culture

    Builds trust, resilience, and meaningful connections

    Leilani leads with compassion, active listening, and a global perspective shaped by international, multicultural leadership. She navigates cultural differences with empathy, shapes culture, connects teams, and builds resilient, inclusive organizations grounded in trust and shared purpose.

  4. 04

    Creativity

    Innovates with intention, impact, and a bias for action

    Leilani is a creative problem-solver who balances today’s priorities with tomorrow’s possibilities. She leads initiatives that turn ideas into measurable outcomes, aligning day-to-day execution with long-term goals. For Leilani, creativity is how organizations adapt, improve, and thrive by turning change into sustainable growth.

  5. 05

    Collaboration

    Aligns people and ideas for shared purpose and results

    Leilani is a natural connector who sees linkages between people, ideas, and opportunities that others may miss. She builds diverse, global teams and identifies high-impact partnerships that unlock individual and organizational potential. A systems thinker, she unites stakeholders around shared goals to drive meaningful results.

  6. 06

    Communication

    Translates vision into clarity, engagement, and inspired action

    With deep experience in the C-suite and boardroom, Leilani communicates with clarity, trust, respect, and authenticity. Whether engaging employees, customers, partners, board members, or investors, she articulates vision, mission, and strategy in ways that inspire confidence and galvanize action.
