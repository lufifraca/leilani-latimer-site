About Leilani Latimer

Learn more about how I lead

Leilani is a strategic C-suite executive, board director, and advisor known for bringing clarity to complexity and aligning bold strategy with practical execution. She partners with CEOs, founders, boards, venture capital, and private equity firms to strengthen growth, improve performance, and guide organizations through critical inflection points.

She brings a founder’s mindset and operator’s discipline to building teams, shaping strategy, and turning ideas into measurable outcomes. Her global leadership experience spans public, PE-backed, venture-funded, and for-purpose organizations, with a consistent track record of delivering results through innovation, collaboration, and sustainable value creation.
